Account & Data Deletion

Balloon Survivors · Studio302Games · Last updated: October 4, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Balloon Survivors account and the data associated with it, both from inside the app and from the web if you no longer have the app installed.

1. Delete your account from inside the app

  1. Open Balloon Survivors.
  2. Go to your Profile (tap your avatar/name), then open the Account section.
  3. Scroll to Delete Account and tap it.
  4. Confirm on the warning screen. This cannot be undone.

Once confirmed, the app immediately deletes your sign-in account and your cloud-saved data (see the table below), then resets the app to a fresh, unregistered state.

2. Request deletion from the web (no app required)

If you no longer have the app installed, or prefer not to use it, you can request deletion by email:

Request deletion by email

Please include in your email:

  • Your in-game display name, and/or
  • The email address you used to sign in (if you created an account), and/or
  • Your approximate last-played date, to help us locate your record.

We will verify and process web-submitted deletion requests, and complete them within a reasonable time, typically within 30 days.

3. What gets deleted

DataDeleted?
Firebase sign-in account (anonymous ID, or email/password, Google/Facebook link)Yes - permanently removed
Cloud-saved progress (level, currency, cards, cosmetics, settings, purchase entitlement flags)Yes - permanently removed
Public leaderboard entry (display name, avatar, best score/time)Yes - permanently removed

4. What is retained, and for how long

We do not retain any personal data after a verified deletion request is completed. We do not keep a separate copy of your progress or account information outside of the systems listed above.

Note: if you made a purchase through Google Play Billing, Google retains its own transaction records as the payment processor, independent of your Balloon Survivors account; those records are governed by Google's own privacy policy, not by us.
