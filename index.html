Privacy Policy

Balloon Survivors · Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Studio302Games ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you play Balloon Survivors (the "App"), available on Google Play.

Developer / Publisher: Studio302Games
Contact email: kartalonurkazak@gmail.com

1. Information We Collect

The App uses Google Firebase to let you keep your progress and compete on leaderboards across devices. The following information is collected and processed:

DataWhat it is / why we collect it
Firebase User ID An anonymous identifier is created automatically the first time you open the App (Firebase Authentication), so your progress can be saved and restored. You are not required to create an account to play.
Email address (optional) If you choose to create an account or link a Google/Facebook sign-in, we store the email address associated with that account so you can recover your progress.
Game progress & settings Level/progress data, in-game currency and items, unlocked cards and cosmetics, purchase entitlements (e.g. "Remove Ads"), and your in-game settings (sound, music, notifications) are stored in our cloud database (Firebase Firestore), linked to your Firebase User ID, so your game state can sync between sessions and devices.
Display name & leaderboard stats If you set a display name, it is shown publicly together with your avatar, best score, and best time on the in-game leaderboard, visible to other players.
Advertising identifier The App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may access your device's advertising ID to serve and measure ads, subject to Google's own privacy practices (see Section 4).
Purchase information In-app purchases (such as "Remove Ads") are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details - Google Play handles payment processing.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos, and the App does not request those permissions.

2. How We Use Information

  • To save and restore your game progress across sessions and devices.
  • To operate optional account sign-in and linked sign-in (Google/Facebook) if you choose to use it.
  • To show your chosen display name, avatar, and scores on public leaderboards.
  • To display advertising and process optional in-app purchases.
  • To maintain and improve the App's functionality and reliability.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers that help us run the App:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore database) - for account sign-in and cloud save.
  • Google AdMob - for displaying in-app advertising.
  • Google Play Billing - for processing in-app purchases.

These providers may process data on servers located outside your country, under Google's own privacy and security practices.

4. Third-Party Privacy Policies

  • Google Play Services / Firebase: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Your game progress is retained for as long as your account/anonymous profile is active, so we can keep your save data available. To request deletion of your data, contact us at kartalonurkazak@gmail.com with your in-game display name or account email, and we will delete your associated cloud data.

6. Children's Privacy

Balloon Survivors is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Security

We use industry-standard services (Google Firebase) with reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
